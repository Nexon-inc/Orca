# ORCA — The Autonomous Business OS

Orca is a state-of-the-art Autonomous OS designed to run businesses via a team of AI Executives. It consolidates management, tech, marketing, and sales into a single, cohesive intelligence layer.

## 🏗️ Core Architecture: The "Core Six"
Orca has been consolidated into 6 primary Executive Agents, each leading a critical business department:
- **ATLAS (CEO / Ops)**: The orchestrator. manages projects, sets mission alignment, and coordinates other executives.
- **ARIA (CMO / Marketing)**: The voice of the brand. Handles SEO, social outreach, and brand positioning.
- **REX (CSO / Sales)**: The revenue driver. Manages pipelines, CRM integrations, and lead conversion.
- **PURITY (CCO / Customer)**: The guardian of experience. Manages support, satisfaction, and user retention.
- **ROMAN (CIO / Intel)**: The brain. Handles deep research, competitive intelligence, and market analysis.
- **GHOST (CTO / Tech)**: The anchor of security. Manages codebases, security audits, and technical scaling.

## 🚀 Instant OS (Orca Hub)
Orca Hub provides pre-configured "Operating System" templates that deploy a specific roster of agents and departments in one click:
- **STARTUP_OS**: Full-stack coverage with all 6 executives.
- **MARKETING_OS**: Focused on brand growth and audience intelligence.
- **E-COMMERCE_OS**: Streamlines digital retail and customer experience.
- **DEV_STUDIO_OS**: Technical-first management for shipping software.
- **CREATOR_OS**: Personal brand growth and audience retention engine.

## 🧠 Smart AI Routing (ORCA Intelligence)
The system uses a highly resilient "Smart Routing" logic for its default intelligence mode:
1. **Gemini 1.5 Pro (Primary)**: Handles all complex reasoning and long-context processing.
2. **Groq (Fallback)**: If Gemini experiences a quota limit, safety block, or network failure, Orca automatically failsover to Groq Llama 3 to ensure your business operations never stop.

## 🛠️ Advanced Features
- **Persistent Memory**: Agents remember past decisions and user preferences, updating their "Recall" block every 10 messages.
- **Model Overrides**: Users can manually select specific elite models (Claude 3.5 Sonnet, GPT-4o, etc.) via the Chat toolbar.
- **Modes**:
  - **Planning**: Deep thought-trace and task breakdown.
  - **Automate**: Rapid execution and task completion.
  - **Approve**: Gates all actions for human review.
