## What is ORCA?

ORCA is an AI Company OS. It gives any company — from a solo founder to a 15-person team — a full AI workforce across 9 departments and 45 named, specialized agents, all coordinated in one dashboard.
ORCA is an AI Solo Founder OS. It gives any founder a full AI workforce across 5 core departments and 25 named, specialized agents, all coordinated in one dashboard.

ORCA is not a chatbot. It is not a prompt tool. It is not another automation layer on top of your existing tools. It is an operating system for your company. Agents have roles, memory, and the ability to coordinate with each other across departments — just like a real team would.

**The problem ORCA solves:**
Most founders are running their entire company alone or with a very small team. They're paying for 8–12 disconnected tools and manually bridging them every day. Hiring a full team across Marketing, Sales, Ops, Finance, Tech, and Hiring costs $300K–$600K per year. So they stay the bottleneck.

ORCA removes that bottleneck by giving them a coordinated AI workforce that works under their control.

## How ORCA Works

### 1. Brief an agent
Open a department. Select an agent. Type your brief or choose a quick prompt.
Agents already know your company — mission, brand voice, ICP, competitors — from your onboarding.

### 2. Agent executes
The agent researches, writes, codes, or analyses. It produces a structured result card with action items and an Approve button.

### 3. Agents coordinate
When a task needs another department, agents hand off work automatically.
Aria (Marketing) finds warm leads → passes them to Rex (Sales) with full context attached.
Rex doesn't start cold. He starts informed.

### 4. You stay in control
Nothing high-stakes executes without your approval. Set each department to:
- **Autopilot** — agents execute and log everything
- **Approve First** — agents prepare, you sign off before anything goes out
- **Suggest Only** — agents draft, you execute manually

## The Coordination System

The cross-department coordination system is ORCA's core mechanic.

When an agent in one department needs to work with an agent in another:

1. The agent flags the need: `[COORDINATION_NEEDED: dept=sales, agent=Rex, reason=warm leads ready]`
2. The Head of the requesting department approves the outgoing request
3. The Head of the receiving department approves the incoming request
4. Both approved → agents execute with full shared context
5. A coordination report is automatically sent to the CEO
6. If no Head is assigned to a department → request escalates directly to the Owner

Every handoff is logged in the real-time Coordination Feed on the Command Center.

**Coordination depth limit:** ORCA enforces a maximum chain depth of 3 hops. If a coordination chain reaches depth 3, it stops and the Owner is notified — preventing infinite loops.

## The Role System

ORCA has 5 human roles. Each sees a completely different dashboard.

### Owner / CEO
- Full access to all 9 departments (plan-gated)
- Command Center with company-wide stats and coordination feed
- Review page: approval queue, department reports, coordination log
- Teams page: invite all role types, assign Department Heads
- Account Settings: all 7 tabs including billing and agent permissions

### Co-founder
- Same as Owner — full dashboard, full team management
- Cannot delete the organisation
- Cannot change the Owner's role

### Department Head
- Scoped to their assigned department only
- Approves all agent tasks within their department
- Approves or rejects incoming cross-department handoff requests
- Submits weekly department report to Owner
- Can invite Members to their own department

### Member
- Scoped to their assigned department only
- Briefs agents freely within their department
- Cross-department requests require Head approval first
- Direct message thread with their Department Head

### Advisor
- Read-only access to department reports and agent activity
- Cannot brief agents or approve anything

## Operating Modes

Set per department in Account Settings → Agent Permissions.

| Mode | What it means |
|------|--------------|
| **Autopilot** | Agents execute automatically. Everything is logged. You review after the fact. |
| **Approve First** | Agents prepare outputs. Nothing executes until you approve. |
| **Suggest Only** | Agents draft suggestions. You decide and execute manually. |

**Master kill switch:** Pause all agents org-wide instantly from Account → Agent Permissions.

## Onboarding

Getting started takes 5 steps — roughly 5 minutes.

**Step 1 — Brief your workforce** *(45 sec)*
Company name, mission, brand voice, ICP. This context is injected into every agent's system prompt.

**Step 2 — Build your org chart** *(1 min)*
Pick which departments to activate. Or install an OrcaHub template and skip this entirely.

**Step 3 — Set operating mode** *(1 min)*
Autopilot, Approve First, or Suggest Only. One global setting to start — adjustable per department later.

**Step 4 — Connect your stack** *(1–2 min)*
Only shows integrations relevant to your chosen departments. Skip and connect later.

**Step 5 — Give your first order** *(1 min)*
Brief your first agent. Your agents already know your company. Watch the result.

## Security

ORCA is a multi-tenant platform. Every organisation's data is completely isolated.

| Layer | Implementation |
|-------|---------------|
| Data at rest | AES-256 (Supabase managed) |
| Integration tokens | AES-256-GCM with authenticated encryption |
| Data in transit | TLS 1.3 |
| Database access | Row Level Security on every table |
| Authentication | Supabase Auth · JWT · 7-day expiry |
| Audit logging | Immutable append-only audit log |
| Rate limiting | Per-user, per-bucket rate limits on all routes |
| Prompt injection | Input scanning + output filtering on every agent call |

**Data promise:** We never sell your data. We never use your agent conversations or company data to train AI models.
